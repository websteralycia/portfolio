A little about me

Former player. Still obsessed with the game.

I've never been able to stay away from basketball. I'm a former collegiate player, and even though my career took me into analytics and technology, I've always found ways to stay involved in the game.

I started Good Game as a way to combine what I love with what I do. It's where I explore basketball through data, build tools, and find ways to turn analytics into something useful for coaches and staff.

What interests me most is learning what matters to the people making decisions and creating resources that fit the way they actually work.
